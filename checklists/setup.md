# Setup checklist: the switch

Tick in order. Chapter references in brackets.

## During the notice period

- [ ] Contract read; the clauses in `09` checked; it is **Deel Contractor**, not EOR (`04`)
- [ ] Group health insurance end date known; conversion offer asked for (`08`)
- [ ] Own **health insurance** bought, starting before the group cover ends (`08`)
- [ ] **Term insurance** applied for while salary slips are current (`08`)
- [ ] F&F statement, relieving letter, payslips saved (`07`)
- [ ] Form 130 delivery e-mail confirmed with HR (`07`)
- [ ] EPF: UAN KYC complete; exit date marked; decision on keep / withdraw noted (`07`)
- [ ] RSU / ESPP status after leaving understood; foreign broker account noted for Schedule FA (`06`, `07`)
- [ ] Work bank account chosen; its **trade-finance / inward remittance desk** e-mail obtained (`05`)
- [ ] CA chosen; scope and fee in writing (`11`)
- [ ] Emergency fund target set: 6 to 12 months of expenses (`08`)

## Before the first invoice

- [ ] Deel account in the legal name on PAN and bank account (`04`)
- [ ] Identity verification complete (`04`)
- [ ] W-8BEN signed, PAN as foreign TIN; PDF saved (`04`)
- [ ] Bank withdrawal method added **and NOC requested at the same time** (`04`, `05`)
- [ ] Purpose code confirmed with Deel support and the bank (`04`)
- [ ] Presumptive or books decided with the CA (`02`)
- [ ] Old or new regime decided; Form 44 noted in the calendar if old (`01`)
- [ ] Rupee value method for USD invoices decided and written in `NOTES.md` (`01`, `03`)
- [ ] GST: register now or at ₹20 lakh, decided (`03`)
- [ ] If registering: GSTIN received; **QRMP** chosen; **LUT filed**, RFD-11 and acknowledgement saved (`03`)
- [ ] Invoice series set (eg `EXP/26-27/001`); template filled with your details (`09`)
- [ ] EDF process asked of the bank (`03`, `05`)
- [ ] Tax set-aside account or fund opened; percentage set (`01`, `08`)
- [ ] Fixed monthly transfer to the personal account set up (`08`)
- [ ] Calendar reminders added from `guide/calendar.md`
- [ ] Client letter confirming the engagement and fee requested, for future loans (`10`)
