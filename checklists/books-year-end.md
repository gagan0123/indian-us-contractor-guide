# Books route: the year-end pack for the CA (and the audit)

For anyone keeping books (`guide/13-keeping-books.md`). Run
`python3 tools/books.py years/<year>` first and clear every flag. Items
marked **audit** are what the tax audit report (Form 26) asks about; they
apply when receipts exceed ₹50 lakh or profit is declared below the
presumptive rate, and help any CA either way.

## A. The registers

- [ ] `sales-register.csv`: every invoice of the year; invoice to bank credit to remittance advice; the rupee method stated
- [ ] `books/expense-register.csv`: every business expense, with head, business share and document
- [ ] `books/fixed-assets.csv`: every asset bought, with put-to-use date and block
- [ ] `books/opening-wdv.csv`: opening written-down value per block, from last year's signed schedule (blank in the first year)
- [ ] `books/summary.md`: generated by the tool, no flags left
- [ ] `books/accounts.csv`: every bank account, card, wallet and broker account in your name, active or not
- [ ] `books/year-end.csv`: balances on 31 March (below)

## B. Documents behind them

- [ ] Bank statements, every account, the whole year, password-free
- [ ] Card statements covering the whole year
- [ ] Every issued invoice; every remittance advice; the contract
- [ ] Expense invoices (`05-expenses/`) and asset invoices
- [ ] GST returns for every period, the LUT; reverse-charge workings
- [ ] AIS, TIS, Form 168; advance-tax challans
- [ ] Last year's return and, if audited, last year's audit report and accounts
- [ ] Form 130 for the salary months (switch year)
- [ ] Investment statements (PPF, mutual funds, deposits, insurance premiums) for the capital account

## C. Balances on 31 March (`books/year-end.csv`)

- [ ] Bank balance of every account
- [ ] Fixed deposits and other investments (cost)
- [ ] Debtors: invoices raised but not yet paid
- [ ] Creditors: business bills received but not yet paid
- [ ] Card dues outstanding
- [ ] Loans taken and given: opening, movement, closing, with a confirmation from the other side
- [ ] Advance tax paid for the year
- [ ] Provision for the CA's fee for the year

## D. Questions the audit report asks (audit)

- [ ] Method of accounting (mercantile or cash) and any change from last year
- [ ] Revenue basis (invoice date) and the foreign-currency conversion method
- [ ] Payments to relatives (salary, rent): who, how much, why reasonable (s. 36(2))
- [ ] Cash payments over ₹10,000 to one person in a day: none (s. 36(4))
- [ ] Loans or deposits taken or repaid in cash, ₹20,000 or more: none (ss. 185, 188); cash received ₹2 lakh or more: none (s. 186)
- [ ] TDS as a payer: you are a "specified person" only if last year's receipts exceeded ₹50 lakh; then rent, contractor and professional payments above the thresholds need TDS (s. 393)
- [ ] Statutory dues (GST, professional tax) paid by their due dates; any delays listed
- [ ] GST: turnover in the books agrees with GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B; differences explained
- [ ] Assets: additions with put-to-use dates, the 180-day split, any sale or scrap
- [ ] Personal expenses charged to the business: none
- [ ] Books kept, and where (electronic, backed up in India)

## E. After the CA signs

- [ ] Signed computation, profit and loss, balance sheet and depreciation schedule saved in `07-income-tax/`
- [ ] Closing written-down values copied into next year's `books/opening-wdv.csv`
- [ ] Audit report accepted on the portal (if audited); return filed and e-verified
- [ ] Decisions taken this year written in `NOTES.md`
