# Year-end checklist

## By 31 December

- [ ] Foreign balances on 31 December and the year's peak recorded: Deel balance, Deel Card, Wise, Payoneer, broker accounts (`06`)
- [ ] Any unreported foreign asset from past years taken to the CA (FAST-DS closes 31 December 2026) (`06`)

## By 15 March

- [ ] Final advance tax paid so at least 100 % of the estimated tax is in (`01`)

## In March

- [ ] **LUT for the next financial year** filed, before the first April invoice (`03`)
- [ ] Next year's invoice series ready
- [ ] Old or new regime for next year reconsidered (remember the one-switch rule) (`01`)

## After 31 March: collect for the CA

- [ ] Form 130 from the former employer (switch year)
- [ ] AIS, TIS and Form 168 downloaded from the e-filing portal
- [ ] Sales register for April to March, complete
- [ ] Every invoice, withdrawal confirmation, NOC, bank advice, EDF acknowledgement
- [ ] Deel transaction history or earnings report for April to March
- [ ] Bank statements of every account, April to March; interest certificates
- [ ] Advance-tax challans
- [ ] GST: all GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B PDFs; the LUT; reverse-charge workings
- [ ] Expense bills and the asset list (if on books)
- [ ] Insurance premium receipts, PPF / NPS / ELSS statements (if old regime)
- [ ] Capital gains statements from brokers and mutual funds
- [ ] Foreign asset values for the calendar year (`06`)
- [ ] `templates/ca-brief.md` filled in

## Filing

- [ ] Computation reviewed: receipts match the sales register; AIS reconciled
- [ ] Form 44 filed by the due date, if choosing the old regime
- [ ] **ITR filed by 31 August** (31 October if audited) and **e-verified** within 30 days
- [ ] ITR acknowledgement and computation saved in `07-income-tax/`
- [ ] Decisions taken this year written in `NOTES.md`
- [ ] Intimation read when it arrives; respond within 30 days to any proposed adjustment (`10`)
