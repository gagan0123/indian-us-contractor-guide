# Quarterly checklist

## Quarter: ______

### GST (if registered, QRMP)

- [ ] All three months' invoices in the sales register, matched to bank credits
- [ ] Reverse-charge purchases totalled (foreign software, any foreign fees)
- [ ] **GSTR-1** filed by the 13th: exports in Table 6A "without payment", HSN/SAC summary, document summary. Check it before filing; 3B cannot correct it
- [ ] GSTR-1A filed, if anything in GSTR-1 was wrong
- [ ] **GSTR-3B** filed by the 22nd / 24th: 3.1(b) from GSTR-1 checked; 3.1(d) reverse charge; Table 4 input credit checked against GSTR-2B
- [ ] Challan paid by net banking (only if reverse charge or a late fee)
- [ ] Filed GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B PDFs and any challan saved in `06-gst/`

### Income tax

- [ ] Year's income re-estimated: salary to date, receipts to date plus expected
- [ ] Tax recomputed (guide page calculator, or the CA)
- [ ] Advance tax paid for the instalment (books route: 15 / 45 / 75 / 100 %; presumptive: as you choose, all by 15 March)
- [ ] Challan saved in `07-income-tax/`
- [ ] Set-aside balance compared with tax still to pay; percentage adjusted

### Housekeeping

- [ ] Every credit in the quarter has its bank advice and EDF; missing ones chased
- [ ] Next quarter's QRMP choice still right (change by the last day of the quarter's first month)
