# Monthly checklist

Copy this block into the year's `NOTES.md` for each month, or tick it here.

## Month: ______

- [ ] Deel invoice generated or submitted; client approved
- [ ] Your GST invoice issued for the cycle and uploaded to Deel with "Replace with custom invoice" (if registered)
- [ ] Withdrawal made; credit seen on the bank statement; UTR noted
- [ ] Tax share moved aside (____ % of the credit)
- [ ] Deel **withdrawal confirmation** downloaded
- [ ] Deel **NOC** downloaded (3 to 4 days after the credit)
- [ ] Bank advice requested from the trade-finance desk, NOC attached (`templates/bank-advice-request.md`)
- [ ] Bank **advice** received and saved; IRM number noted
- [ ] **EDF** filed with the bank for this month's invoice, within 30 days after month end
- [ ] Sales register row complete: invoice no., date, Deel invoice, USD, INR value, credit date, UTR, advice
- [ ] Any foreign software bought for work noted for reverse charge (if registered)
- [ ] Business expense bills saved (if on books, or for input credit)
- [ ] Fixed transfer to the personal account made

The set for the month, in `years/<year>/`: the invoice (yours, if registered),
withdrawal confirmation, NOC, bank advice, EDF acknowledgement, statement.
