# Indian US Contractor Guide

A guide, checklists and a records skeleton for a salaried professional in
India who becomes an **independent contractor to a foreign client**, paid
in rupees through **Deel**. Written for **tax year 2026-27**, the first
year under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

**Start with [`guide/00-start-here.md`](guide/00-start-here.md).** The
interactive companion page (tax estimator, calendar, setup checklist) is
`page/guide.html`.

## Use it

This is a GitHub template. Click **Use this template → Create a new
repository**, make it **private**, and keep your documents in your copy
under `years/`. Never put PAN, account numbers or statements in a public
repository.

## Accuracy

**Written as at October 2026, for tax year 2026-27.** Each rule cites the
old and new section and a source; a few rest on secondary sources and
`guide/sources.md` says which. Law, rates, forms and portal screens change
every year, and points marked **"for your CA"** are judgement calls. This
guide helps you understand and ask; it is not tax, legal or financial
advice. Have a CA confirm anything that decides a figure on your return.
