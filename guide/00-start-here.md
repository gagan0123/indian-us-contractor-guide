# Start here: from employee to contractor

You are moving from a salary to a contract with a US company, paid in
rupees through Deel. Legally you become a **self-employed professional**
running a one-person export business. This guide is the map. Read this
page, then the chapters in the order your first month needs them.

## What changes

| | As an employee | As a contractor |
|---|---|---|
| Who you work for | an employer | a client, under a business contract |
| Income tax is on | salary | **profit of your profession** (`01`, `02`) |
| Tax is paid | by your employer, monthly (TDS) | **by you**, as advance tax (`01`) |
| Return form | ITR-1 or ITR-2, due 31 July | **ITR-4 or ITR-3, due 31 August** (31 October if audited) |
| Regime choice | each year | once you have business income, **switching is limited** (`01`) |
| GST | none | **register above ₹20 lakh** (optional below), file an LUT, two returns a quarter (`03`) |
| Proof of income | Form 16, payslips | invoices, bank advices, your ITRs (`05`, `10`) |
| PF, gratuity | employer pays in | stops; you replace it (`07`, `08`) |
| Health and life cover | group policy | **buy your own before you leave** (`08`) |
| Paid leave, notice pay | yes | no, unless the contract says so (`08`, `09`) |
| Foreign money | none | anything held on Deel, Wise or a foreign card goes in **Schedule FA** (`06`) |

## Your first year, in order

**Before your last working day**
1. Read and negotiate the contract (`09`). Confirm it is **Deel
   Contractor**, not Deel EOR.
2. Buy **health and term insurance**, or convert the group policy (`08`).
3. Collect Form 130 details, the F&F statement, EPF details (`07`).
4. Open or choose **one bank account for the work**, with a bank whose
   trade-finance desk you can reach (`05`).
5. Find a **CA** who handles freelancers and exporters (`11`).

**Before your first invoice**
6. Set up Deel: verification, W-8BEN, bank method **with the NOC
   requested**, purpose code (`04`).
7. Decide **GST**: register or not. If registering: apply, then file the
   **LUT** before the first invoice (`03`).
8. Decide with the CA: **presumptive or books** (`02`), and which rupee
   value you will report for a USD invoice.

**Every month** (`checklists/monthly.md`)
9. Invoice, withdraw, download the confirmation and NOC, request the
   bank's advice, file the EDF, file the set, move the tax share aside.

**Every quarter** (`checklists/quarterly.md`)
10. GSTR-1 by the 13th, GSTR-3B by the 22nd or 24th. Advance tax by 15
    June, September, December, March (or all by 15 March if presumptive).

**After the year ends** (`checklists/year-end.md`)
11. Collect Form 130, AIS, Form 168, Deel statements, 31 December foreign
    balances; brief the CA; file the ITR by **31 August**; renew the LUT
    before the next April invoice.

## How to use this repo

- `guide/` explains; read it once and come back to it.
- `checklists/` are what you tick each month, quarter and year.
- `templates/` are the invoice, the bank e-mail, the CA brief and the
  sales register.
- `years/` is a skeleton for your own documents. Click **Use this
  template** on GitHub to make your own **private** copy before adding
  anything real.
- `guide/calendar.md` is the year at a glance; `guide/glossary.md`
  explains the jargon.

## About accuracy

Written in October 2026 for **tax year 2026-27**, the first year under the
**Income-tax Act, 2025**. Each rule cites its section in both the old and
the new Act, with sources in `guide/sources.md`. Law, rates, forms and
portal screens change every year, and some points here are judgement
calls marked **"for your CA"**. Use this to understand your situation and
ask better questions; have a CA confirm anything that decides a figure
on your return.
