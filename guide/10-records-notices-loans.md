# Records, notices and proof of income

## What to keep, and for how long

| Law | Keep for |
|---|---|
| Income tax, books of a profession (Rule 46(9) of the Income-tax Rules, 2026) | **7 tax years** from the end of the tax year (one more than under the old rules) |
| GST (CGST s. 36) | 72 months from the due date of the annual return for the year (about 7 years) |
| Reopening of income-tax assessments | can reach back several years, longer where income escaped is large; another reason to keep everything |

Simplest rule: **keep every year for at least 8 years**, and keep the
records of foreign assets and capital purchases (a laptop's invoice)
longer.

## A folder per year

The `years/` skeleton in this repo is one way to lay it out:

```
years/2026-27/
  00-employment/      Form 130, payslips, F&F statement, relieving letter
  01-contract/        the signed contract, amendments, W-8BEN
  02-invoices/        Deel invoices and your GST invoices, by month
  03-remittance/      withdrawal confirmations, NOCs, bank advices, EDFs
  04-bank/            monthly statements of the work account
  05-expenses/        bills (only if on books, or for GST input credit)
  06-gst/             RFD-11 and acknowledgement, each GSTR-1 and 3B PDF, challans
  07-income-tax/      advance-tax challans, AIS, Form 168, the filed ITR and acknowledgement
  08-foreign-assets/  31 December and peak balances, broker statements
  09-insurance-investments/  premium receipts, PPF/NPS statements
  10-correspondence/  e-mails with the bank, Deel support, the CA, any notice
  books/              books route only: expense, asset and year-end registers (`13-keeping-books.md`)
  sales-register.csv
  NOTES.md            decisions taken this year and why
```

Name files so they sort by date: `2026-09-05 - NOC - <bank> - <UTR>.pdf`.
Keep the repo **private** if you put real documents in it, and keep a
second copy (an encrypted drive or cloud folder) in case of loss.

## Notices: what they are and what to do

| Notice | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Intimation** (old s. 143(1), now s. 270(1)) | The processing result of your return; most say "no demand, no refund" or show a refund | Read it. If it proposes an adjustment, you get a communication first: **respond within 30 days** on the portal (Pending Actions → e-Proceedings), agreeing or giving reasons |
| A demand in an intimation | Tax the system thinks you owe | If it is wrong, file a **rectification** (old s. 154, now s. 287) under Services → Rectification; if right, pay within 30 days |
| **Defective return** (old s. 139(9), now s. 263(7)) | Something is missing or inconsistent (often: receipts lower than in AIS, or a missing schedule in ITR-3) | Fix and re-upload within **15 days**, or the return is treated as never filed |
| **Scrutiny** (old s. 143(2), now s. 270(8)) | A detailed assessment | Involve your CA from the first day |
| GST **ASMT-10** or a mismatch notice | A difference between your returns, or with GSTR-2B | Reply on the portal within the time given, with your sales register and documents |

Notices arrive on the e-filing and GST portals and by e-mail to the
registered address. **Real notices never ask for payment to a personal
account or by phone**; check any e-mail by logging into the portal
yourself. Keep your e-mail and mobile current on both portals.

## Proof of income for loans, cards and visas

Without salary slips, lenders and consulates ask for:

- **ITRs for two or three years**, with the acknowledgement (ITR-V) and
  computation of income. Your first contractor ITR is only available
  after the year ends, so plan large loans accordingly: apply while
  employed, or wait a year.
- **Bank statements** for 6 to 12 months showing regular credits. A
  separate work account with a fixed monthly transfer to yourself looks
  like salary to an underwriter.
- **The contract** and a letter from the client confirming the
  engagement and fee (ask for one when you start; it is easy then).
- **GST registration certificate** and recent returns, if registered.
- A **CA-certified statement of income** for the current year, for an
  application mid-year.
- **Form 168 / 26AS** showing taxes paid.

Some banks treat self-employed professionals with a two-year history as
standard. With less, expect a lower limit or a co-applicant.
