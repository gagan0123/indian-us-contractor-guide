# Compliance calendar: tax year 2026-27

For a contractor on QRMP (quarterly GST). The GSTR-3B date is the 22nd or
the 24th depending on your state (`03-gst.md`); this calendar shows both.
A date on a weekend or holiday is not automatically extended; file early.

## Every month

| When | What |
|---|---|
| On each Deel credit | move the tax share aside; download the withdrawal confirmation and NOC when ready |
| First week after the NOC | e-mail the bank for the inward advice |
| Within 30 days after the end of the invoice month | **EDF** with your bank (FEMA, from 1 October 2026) |
| 25th of months 1 and 2 of a quarter | **PMT-06**, only if you owe GST (eg reverse charge) |

## Recurring, not tied to the year

| When | What |
|---|---|
| End of the third calendar year after signing | Renew the W-8BEN (or sooner if your details change) |
| Policy anniversary | Renew health and term insurance |
| Each April | Restart the invoice series; new LUT in place |
