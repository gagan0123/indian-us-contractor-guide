# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Advance tax** | Income tax paid during the year by instalments, by you, instead of TDS |
| **AIS / TIS** | Annual Information Statement / Taxpayer Information Summary: what the tax department already knows about you (interest, TDS, large transactions). On the e-filing portal |
| **ARN** | Application Reference Number, the acknowledgement of a GST filing or LUT |
| **BIRC / FIRA / FIRC** | Bank documents proving money came from abroad (`05-inward-remittance.md`) |
| **CA** | Chartered Accountant |
| **Deel Contractor / Deel EOR** | Contractor: you invoice the client through Deel. EOR (Employer of Record): Deel employs you on payroll |
| **EDF** | Export Declaration Form, filed with your bank for each month's export invoices from 1 October 2026 |
| **Form 44** | Option to use the old tax regime when you have business income (was Form 10-IEA) |
| **Form 130** | Salary TDS certificate from your employer (was Form 16) |
| **Form 168** | Annual tax credit statement (was Form 26AS) |
| **GSTIN** | Your 15-character GST registration number |
| **GSTR-1** | GST return of your outward supplies (sales) |
| **GSTR-3B** | GST summary return and payment |
| **IGST** | Integrated GST, the tax on inter-state supplies and exports |
| **ITC** | Input tax credit: GST paid on purchases, usable against output tax or refundable for exporters |
| **ITR-3 / ITR-4** | Income-tax return forms for business or professional income; ITR-4 is the short form for the presumptive scheme |
| **LUT** | Letter of Undertaking (form RFD-11): lets you export without paying IGST |
| **NOC** | No Objection Certificate from Deel's correspondent bank, needed for your bank's advice |
| **Presumptive scheme** | Old s. 44ADA, now s. 58: 50 % of receipts deemed profit |
| **Purpose code** | RBI code for the reason of a remittance (eg P0802) |
| **QRMP** | Quarterly Return Monthly Payment scheme for GST |
| **RCM** | Reverse charge: you pay GST on a service you buy, eg from a foreign supplier |
| **SAC** | Services Accounting Code, the classification on your invoice (998314, 998313) |
| **Schedule FA** | Foreign assets schedule of the ITR |
| **SNRR / Rupee Vostro** | Rupee accounts that non-residents and foreign banks hold with Indian banks |
| **Tax year** | Under the 2025 Act, the financial year itself (1 April to 31 March); replaces "previous year" and "assessment year" |
| **TDS** | Tax deducted at source |
| **UTR** | Unique Transaction Reference of an RTGS/NEFT payment |
| **W-8BEN** | US form certifying you are not a US person |
