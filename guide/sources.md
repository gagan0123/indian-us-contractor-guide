# Sources

Checked between September and October 2026. **Primary** means the
statute, rule, notification or circular text itself (directly or as
reproduced section by section); **secondary** means commentary. Where a
chapter relies on secondary sources only, it says so.

## Income tax

- Income-tax Act, 2025, reproduced section by section:
  `eztax.in/income-tax-act-2025/section-<n>` for ss. 19, 28, 33, 34, 36,
  57, 58, 62, 63, 123, 124, 126, 156, 159, 202, 263, 266, 267, 270, 287,
  289, 392, 393, 404 to 411, 423 to 428, 516. Primary.
- Finance Bill 2026 and Memorandum (due date to 31 August, s. 428 fees,
  revised-return window, Black Money Act prosecution carve-out, FAST-DS):
  `indiabudget.gov.in/doc/Finance_Bill.pdf`, `/doc/memo.pdf`. Primary
  (the enacted Finance Act, 2026, Act 4 of 2026, received assent on 30
  March 2026; its text was not compared clause by clause with the Bill).
- Income-tax Act, 2025, s. 17(1)(d) (specified securities from a current
  or former employer): eztax.in/income-tax-act-2025/section-17. Primary.
- Income-tax Rules, 2026, Rule 46 (books, seven-year retention):
  `lawspedia.in/rule-46-of-income-tax-rules-2026`.
- Old-to-new section maps: cleartax.in (income-tax-act-2025 section
  numbers), taxgarden.in. Secondary.
- Form 130 and Form 168: incometaxindia.gov.in Form 130 FAQs;
  taxguru.in; carajput.com. Secondary.
- ITR-4 eligibility (AY 2026-27 forms): taxguru.in, studycafe.in,
  scconline.com. Secondary; the forms for tax year 2026-27 were not yet
  notified.
- EPF, gratuity, leave encashment: incometaxindia.gov.in (taxability of
  retirement benefits), taxguru.in, basunivesh.com, businesstoday.in.
  Secondary.
- Black Money Act ₹20 lakh carve-out: taxguru.in, taxmann.com,
  studycafe.in. Secondary; Finance Bill 2026 for prosecution, primary.
- FAST-DS 2026: Finance Bill 2026 (scheme text); outlookmoney.com,
  tribuneindia.com (dates). Mixed.
- India-US tax treaty, Article 15: `irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/india.pdf`.
  Primary.
