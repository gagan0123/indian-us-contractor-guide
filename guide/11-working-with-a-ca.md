# Working with a CA

## Do you need one?

On the presumptive scheme, with no foreign assets and no GST
registration, a careful person can file ITR-4 alone. Most contractors
still use a CA, because the first year has several judgement calls
(listed at the end of each chapter), GST adds quarterly filings, and a
mistake found three years later costs far more than the fee.

## Choosing one

Ask before engaging:

- Do you have **freelancer or remote-contractor clients** paid from
  abroad? How many?
- Do you handle **GST for exporters**: LUTs, zero-rated returns, refunds
  of unutilised credit?
- Have you worked with **Deel, Upwork or Payoneer** payouts, and with the
  NOC and inward-advice process?
- Are you working under the **Income-tax Act, 2025** numbering and the
  new forms?
- What is included in the fee: ITR, GST returns each quarter, LUT,
  advance-tax computations, notice replies? What is extra?
- How do you want documents: a shared folder, e-mail, a portal?
- Who files: you on my login, or me with your computation? (Never share
  your portal password by message; if the CA files, use the portal's
  authorised-representative or CA-access features where they exist, and
  change passwords afterwards.)

Typical fees vary widely by city. Get the scope in writing.

## What to send, and when

| When | Send |
|---|---|
| Once, at the start | PAN, Aadhaar, contract, W-8BEN, bank details, GST registration and LUT, last year's ITR and Form 16 |
| Each quarter, before the 10th of the month after | the sales register, the quarter's invoices and remittance advices, any reverse-charge purchases; for advance tax, your income estimate |
| After 31 March | `checklists/year-end.md`; `templates/ca-brief.md` as the cover note |

## Questions to settle in the first year

Collected from the chapters:

1. Profession or business, and presumptive or books (`02`).
2. Old or new regime, and whether to file Form 44 (`01`).
3. Whether the first-year interest relief under s. 425(4) applies (`01`).
4. The rupee value for a USD invoice, and invoice date or credit date
   (`01`, `03`, `04`).
5. Register for GST now or at ₹20 lakh (`03`).
6. Anything needed for rupee credits before Circular 263/09/2026 (26
   October 2026), if a refund is claimed for those months (`03`).
7. Your GST invoice uploaded to Deel in place of its standard invoice
   (`03`, `09`).
8. The EDF process with your bank (`03`, `05`).
9. Schedule FA: which platform balances to report (`06`).
10. EPF: keep, withdraw or move; tax on interest after leaving (`07`).
11. The W-8BEN treaty line (`04`).

Write each answer, with the date and who decided it, in the year's
`NOTES.md`. Next year you inherit the decisions instead of re-asking.
