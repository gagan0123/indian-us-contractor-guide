If the client is your employer's group

Same company, new paper

Often the move is from the Indian entity's payroll to a contract with the US entity of the same group. Three things need care.

Who receives the work?

An export needs the recipient outside India. Where a client has an Indian establishment, it is the one "most directly concerned with the receipt" (IGST s. 2(14)). Work really done for the Indian entity is not an export: 18 % GST. Contract with, bill and get paid by the US entity, for its business.

RSUs and options

Shares from awards granted while employed stay a salary perquisite even after you leave: s. 17(1)(d) covers the "former employer". Nobody withholds tax on them now, so add them to your advance tax. Ask the plan administrator in writing what happens to unvested awards.

The Indian exit is still an exit

Gratuity, leave encashment and Form 130 come from the Indian entity; EPF and group health cover stop. Get a service letter from the Indian entity and an engagement letter from the US entity together.

    GST

    Zero tax, real paperwork

    Work for a US client is an export of services, and exports are zero-rated. Once registered with a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) on file, you charge no GST and pay none on your fees. What GST costs is an invoice per fee, two returns a quarter and an LUT each April.

    Must you register?

    Optional while contract fees in the financial year stay at or under ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in Manipur, Mizoram, Nagaland, Tripura), thanks to Notification 10/2017-IT. Above it, mandatory within 30 days. Salary does not count; export fees do. No composition scheme for exporters.

    Is it an export? (IGST s. 2(6))

    1. You are in India
    2. The client is outside India
    3. Place of supply outside India: the client's location
    4. Paid in foreign exchange, or in rupees the RBI permits
    5. Not branches of one entity

    Deel pays rupees out of a correspondent's Rupee Vostro account. CBIC Circular 263/09/2026-GST (26 October 2026, para 2.2) names "INR Nostro (Rupee Vostro) of correspondent banks" and says "Rupee Vostro and SNRR are permissible accounts for receipt of export proceeds". That is the Deel route.

    The LUT

    • Form RFD-11 on gst.gov.in, under User Services
    • Accepted the moment the ARN appears
    • One financial year; renew in March
    • File before the first export invoice

    Your invoice (CGST Rule 46)

    • Your GSTIN, a serial number unique in the year
    • Client name, address, country
    • SAC 998314 (development) or 998313 (consulting)
    • "SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT UNDER LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"
    • LUT ARN and date

    Deel lets you upload your own invoice in place of its standard one: Finance → Invoices → Replace with custom invoice.

    Returns under QRMP

    • GSTR-1 by the 13th after the quarter; exports in Table 6A
    • GSTR-3B by the 22nd or 24th, by state; Table 3.1(b)
    • 3B takes its sales from GSTR-1 and cannot be edited: get GSTR-1 right
    • No GSTR-9 below ₹2 crore

    New RBI rules, 1 October 2026

    • Realise each invoice within 9 months, or 12 if invoiced or settled in rupees
    • File an EDF with your bank within 30 days of the end of the invoice month

    FEMA 23(R)/2026-RB. Ask your bank how it takes an EDF for a Deel rupee credit.

    Deel and your bank

    One credit, five documents

    A Deel rupee payout arrives as a domestic RTGS from Deel's Indian correspondent bank, so your bank never sees a foreign wire and issues no remittance advice by itself. Here is the routine that gets you one, every month.

    Withdrawal methods

    INR bank transfer ($0 Deel fee) is the default, the only method Deel issues the NOC for, and leaves nothing abroad. SWIFT costs $5 to $10. A Deel balance, Deel Card, Wise or Payoneer holds money outside India: report it in Schedule FA, which also rules out ITR-4.

    W-8BEN

    The US form saying you are not a US person. PAN goes in the foreign TIN field. Work done in India is not US-source income, so no US tax is withheld. For your CA: the treaty line in Part II. Renew by the end of the third calendar year.

    Purpose code

    Deel sends INR payouts as "Software consultancy/implementation (other than those covered in SOFTEX form)", code P0802. If your bank asks what the credit is, say that, never "salary"; some banks ask for the contract and invoices first.

    Protection and cash flow

    Health cover

    Group cover ends with the job. Ask HR about converting to an individual policy within the window; otherwise buy before the end date, so waiting periods start early. A base policy plus a super top-up is the cheap way to a large cover.

    Term life

    Apply while you still have payslips. Insurers underwrite the self-employed on ITRs, and your first contractor ITR is a year away.

    Retirement

    PF stops. PPF (₹1.5 lakh a year), NPS, and a monthly SIP sized to what PF took (about 24 % of basic) replace the habit. EPF already saved: keep it, or withdraw tax-free after five years' service.

    Cash flow

    One account for the work, a fixed monthly transfer to yourself, the set-aside percentage from the estimator moved on every credit, and an emergency fund of 6 to 12 months: a contract can end on short notice with no severance.
